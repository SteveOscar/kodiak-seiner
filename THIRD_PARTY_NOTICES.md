# Third-party notices

## Elevation data

Terrain and bathymetry of the Kodiak archipelago are derived from **Terrain Tiles** on the Registry of Open Data on
AWS (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), produced by Mapzen / the Linux Foundation (Tilezen project).
Source attribution per https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md, including:

- 3DEP (formerly NED) and topobathy courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey
- ETOPO1 courtesy of the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
- GMTED2010 courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey
- SRTM courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey / NASA

The game resamples and rescales this data (`tools/fetch-dem.mjs`); it is not suitable for navigation.
